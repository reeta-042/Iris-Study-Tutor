---
name: IRIS prototype scope
description: User-defined audience, prototype boundaries, and accessibility constraints for IRIS.
---

IRIS is a voice-first study tutor for visually impaired and low-vision university students in Nigeria. This deliverable is a clickable Android portrait mockup for aligning a development team, not production.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined the audience and prototype purpose.

**How to apply:** Keep voice, camera coaching, and notes interactions simulated unless the user requests implementation. Do not add login or menus. Preserve dark backgrounds, white text, #FFD400 as the single bright accent, AAA text contrast targets, very large type, and touch targets at least 64px. Include screen-reader labels, focus order, audio cues, and vibration specifications outside the student-facing UI.
