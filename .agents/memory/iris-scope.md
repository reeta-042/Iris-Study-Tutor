---
name: IRIS prototype scope
description: User-defined audience, prototype boundaries, and accessibility constraints for IRIS.
---

IRIS is a voice-first study tutor for visually impaired and low-vision university students in Nigeria. This deliverable is a clickable Android portrait mockup for aligning a development team, not production.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined the audience and prototype purpose.

**How to apply:** Keep voice, camera coaching, and notes interactions simulated unless the user requests implementation. Do not add login or menus. Preserve deep navy backgrounds, white text, pink secondary accents, AAA text contrast targets, very large type, and touch targets at least 64px. The user replaced the original yellow palette with navy and pink. Keep screen-reader labels, focus order, audio cues, and vibration specifications available on demand without cluttering the study screen.

The user approved the abstract breathing, moving AI orb with heartbeat-like talking pulses as the default avatar. While listening, after a few seconds it should briefly morph into an eye, pop forward and look out, then return to the orb; the eye must not be the default. Keep a small translucent rounded rectangle with a glowing border immediately below it. Tapping the rectangle should open an on-screen popup for captions or accessibility details. The voice action should be a circular call-style button only when needed, not a Talk to Iris button while Iris is talking.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a more intuitive experience and this visual treatment rather than permanently visible accessibility notes.

**How to apply:** Keep the glass treatment subtle but its text readable, preserve large touch targets, and make the popup dismissible and keyboard accessible. Use the luminous AI visual as the focal point without implying actual sentience.
