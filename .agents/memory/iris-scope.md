---
name: IRIS prototype scope
description: User-defined audience, prototype boundaries, and accessibility constraints for IRIS.
---

IRIS is a voice-first study tutor for visually impaired and low-vision university students in Nigeria. This deliverable is a clickable Android portrait mockup for aligning a development team, not production.

**Why:** The user explicitly defined the audience and prototype purpose.

**How to apply:** Keep voice, camera coaching, and notes interactions simulated unless the user requests implementation. Do not add login or menus. Preserve dark backgrounds, white text, #FFD400 as the single bright accent, AAA text contrast targets, very large type, and touch targets at least 64px. Keep screen-reader labels, focus order, audio cues, and vibration specifications available on demand without cluttering the study screen.

The user wants the voice agent to be a glowing circular grid, with a small translucent rounded rectangle and glowing border immediately below it. Tapping the rectangle should open an on-screen popup for captions or accessibility details.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested a more intuitive experience and this visual treatment rather than permanently visible accessibility notes.

**How to apply:** Keep the glass treatment subtle but its text readable, preserve large touch targets, and make the popup dismissible and keyboard accessible. Use the luminous AI visual as the focal point without implying actual sentience.
